JIDAI SHIFT - audio asset credits and licences
============================================================

Every file under assets/audio/ is either:
  (a) derived from a Kenney audio pack - Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
      https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/  (credit 'Kenney / www.kenney.nl' is optional),
      decoded, trimmed, normalised, re-encoded and in some cases processed (e.g. running-footstep variants);
  (b) synthesised procedurally by tools/audio/*.py (numpy/scipy, no recorded samples) for this project.
      These synthesised sounds and the music are released under CC0 1.0 as well.

Regenerate: python3 tools/audio/build.py --kenney <dir with the extracted Kenney packs>
No freesound.org / OpenGameArt / Sonniss material is included.
